\begin{abstract}
We benchmark published self-supervised objectives---JEPA, InfoNCE, VICReg,
SIGReg, uniformity, and SwAV---on a fixed $\sim$3M-parameter SmallResNet
with matched masking, augmentation, and evaluation on CIFAR-100.
Dual InfoNCE on mask and augmentation paths yields strong k-NN accuracy
($\sim$0.22) but weak linear probes ($\sim$0.10).
We propose \emph{asymmetric hybrids} that retain InfoNCE on the masked
predictor path while replacing augmentation InfoNCE with MSE plus either
decomposed variance/covariance penalties or LeJEPA-style SIGReg.
At 100 epochs the SIGReg hybrid reaches linear accuracy 0.154--0.175 depending on
training length (best 0.175 @ epoch~390 in a 418-epoch continuation), with k-NN
0.179--0.193 for the SmallResNet hybrids.
A unified 200-epoch ablation and k-NN/linear trade-off plots situate our
method among standard loss families under identical compute and architecture.
\end{abstract}
